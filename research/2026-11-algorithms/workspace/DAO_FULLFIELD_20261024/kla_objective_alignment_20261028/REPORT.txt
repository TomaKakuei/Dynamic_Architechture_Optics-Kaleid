What the KLA code actually optimizes
Source: Adaptive Optics/ADAPTIVE_OPTICS_V15P/kla_joint_sensorless_20261008.
Network receives one noisy9-field camera packet and predicts40 normalized lens
coordinates. It does NOT backpropagate MTF. Physical training loss is mean
ell/(1+ell), ell=log1p((PT WRMS/0.07)^2), plus0.05 inverse-pose SmoothL1 and10
mechanical penalty. Defocus is retained at a fixed image plane. Original Q2000
is mean of9 tangential MTF values at2000lp/mm minus0.3 population SD, pass>=0.45.
It is a single spatial-frequency/direction score, not a full2D PSF match.
